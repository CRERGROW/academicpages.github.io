## Biography

I am **Hsu Cheng Kang**, a Master's student at **National Sun Yat-sen University (NSYSU)**, Taiwan. 

My research focuses on **Large Language Model (LLM) Security**, **Prompt Injection & Jailbreak Defense**, and **Adversarial Machine Learning**. I am particularly interested in designing inference-time adaptation frameworks and runtime guardrails that protect AI systems against evolving adversarial threats without retraining model parameters.
